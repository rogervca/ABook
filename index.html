Chapter 1: Can a Single Hair Cell Start a New Life? (FREE)

Inside the root of a single hair sits a complete biological instruction manual—your DNA. By introducing genetic switches to an ordinary skin or hair cell, scientists can rewind its clock into an induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC).

Researchers have already turned ordinary mouse skin cells into functional gametes that grew into healthy baby mice, proving every body cell holds an extraordinary blueprint.

Chapter 2: The First Week "Handshake" & Smart Fluid (FREE)

Around Day 5 after fertilization, a hollow ball of cells called a blastocyst runs out of starter fuel and urgently needs a lifeline. Its outer cells perform a delicate chemical "handshake" with the uterine lining to build the placenta.

In an external life-support capsule, labs manufacture bio-identical hormones (like progesterone) and dissolve them into warm, oxygenated nutrient fluid.
